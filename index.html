ClipScope

Creator analytics for your own TikTok channel.

Understand what works

ClipScope is a desktop analytics app designed to help creators review their own TikTok account and public video performance, compare content, and identify useful patterns.

TikTok connection

ClipScope is designed to connect only after the creator authorizes access through TikTok's official login flow. The app does not ask users to share their TikTok password directly.

Privacy-first

The planned desktop version stores analytics and authorization data locally on the user's computer whenever possible.
